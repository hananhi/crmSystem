CRM Project

Overview:
Lead Tracking and Management: Centralizes all lead information, tracking interactions, packages and activities, ensuring every potential customer is nurtured through the sales pipeline effectively.
Calendar and Meeting Management: Integrates with calendars for scheduling and tracking meetings with leads, enabling automated follow-ups and reminders to enhance engagement and prevent opportunities from slipping through the cracks.

Features:
leads Management.
Automatic whatsApp reminder before 10 min of the meeting with the customer.
Sales Pipeline.
Reporting and Dashboards.
Impot excel of leads.
Meetings managment and followUps.
Support three languages (English , Hebrew , Arabic).
